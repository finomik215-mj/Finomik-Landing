FAQ

What schools
ask us.

What is Finomik?

Finomik is a gamified financial education platform for schools. Students practise adult-life decisions with challenges, simulation and artificial intelligence, and the teacher follows the whole class's progress.

Which year groups is it for?

Secondary school. Adult life, Investing and Entrepreneurship work for any year group; Economics is for the final years.

How many hours does it need?

Between 1 and 2 hours a week for a term. The programme adapts to the hours you have.

Does the teacher need to be an expert?

No. The platform can explain the theory on its own, and the teacher decides whether to teach the class and use Finomik just for the activities.

Is the AI safe for students?

The assistant only answers questions about financial education, adapts its explanation to the student's level, and the teacher can switch it off for their class.

Where is the data stored?

On servers in the European Union, in Germany. There are no ads and no data is sold.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of students and programmes. We'll go through it in a 20-minute demo.

Which languages is it in?

Spanish and English.

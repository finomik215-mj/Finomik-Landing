Cookie policy.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This website doesn't use cookies

finomik.com doesn't place analytics, advertising or social media cookies in your browser. That's why we don't show you a banner asking you to accept them.

How we measure visits

We use Vercel Web Analytics, which works without cookies: it counts page views, country and device type in aggregate, without storing anything in your browser and without identifying you.

Our own resources

Fonts, animations and videos are served from our own domain. Visiting the website doesn't send data to Google or other external providers.

If this changes

If we ever use cookies that aren't strictly necessary, we'll update this page and ask for your consent before placing them. For any questions, write to hello@finomik.com.

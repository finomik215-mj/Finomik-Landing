About us

We teach how to decide.

Finomik is a financial education platform for schools. We exist so that no student reaches their first salary, first contract or first investment without knowing what they're deciding.

Why we exist

Nobody explains this at school.
Until now.

Almost 1 in 5 15-year-olds don't reach the baseline level of financial literacy (PISA 2022). And the decisions come anyway: rent, instalments, subscriptions, offers that look like a good deal. We want your students to face them with judgement.

How we do it

Practice, not memorisation.

Learning by decidingReal situations, challenges, an investment simulator and an AI tutor that explains things to each student at their level.
The teacher decidesTeach the class or let the platform explain. See every student's progress and control what content the class uses.
Protected dataData stored in the European Union, in Germany. No ads and no selling of data.

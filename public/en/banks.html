The problem

A lack of financial education
creates friction.

Many clients don't take up banking products because they don't understand them, not because they aren't interested. They don't get the product, so they turn it down. Finomik educates clients in context, inside the bank's own platform, so they arrive informed and confident.

Without financial education

  • The client doesn't understand the products and rejects them
  • A purely transactional, distant relationship
  • Opportunities lost to friction

With Finomik

  • The client understands and approaches products with judgement
  • The bank becomes an ally in their financial growth
  • Better conversion and less friction when deciding
